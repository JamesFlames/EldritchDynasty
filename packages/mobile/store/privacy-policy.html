Eldritch Dynasty — Privacy Policy

Effective date: 2026-09-20

This app collects no personal data and transmits nothing anywhere. That is not a promise about intent; it is a statement about what the app is built to do, and you can check it: Eldritch Dynasty makes no network requests. There is no login, no account, no analytics library, and no crash-reporting library built into it.

What the app does with your data

Nothing, because it never leaves your device. Everything the game needs — its rules, its authored events, its world — is built into the app itself before it ever reaches you. The game runs the same whether or not the device has a network connection.

Saved games

Your progress is saved locally, on your own device, in storage that belongs to the app. It is never uploaded, synced, or shared. If you uninstall the app, that save is gone unless you have exported it yourself.

What Google Play measures

Google Play's own operator tooling (Android vitals) may record standard, aggregated technical signals about how the app runs on your device — such as whether it crashed or became unresponsive — as part of operating the Play Store. That collection is Google's, under Google's own policies, and this app does not add any collection of its own on top of it.

Children

This app is not directed at children and is not part of Google Play's Families program.

Changes to this policy

If the app ever starts collecting or transmitting data — for example, if a future version adds cloud saves or achievements — this page will be updated first, and the change will be reflected in the app's Play Store data safety declaration before that version ships.
